# Fleas, ticks and worms

Regular parasite prevention protects pets and the people they live with.

- **Fleas** cause itching and can pass on tapeworms. Indoor pets can still get them.
- **Ticks** can carry serious diseases. Check dogs after walks in grass or woodland.
- **Worms** are common, especially in young animals and pets that hunt.

**Use products made for the right species and weight.** Some dog flea treatments contain permethrin, which can cause tremors and seizures in cats. If a cat has been treated with a dog product, **contact a vet now.**

Ask your vet how often to treat; it depends on the product and your area.
