# Healthy weight

Extra weight raises the risk of arthritis, diabetes, and (in cats) urinary problems. It also shortens life.

**Quick check:** you should be able to feel the ribs easily under a thin layer of fat, and see a waist from above.

**To help a pet lose weight:**

- weigh food rather than using a scoop
- count treats as part of the daily food, and keep them under about 10% of it
- increase activity gradually
- aim for slow loss; a vet can set a safe target

Cats must lose weight slowly. Cutting food sharply can make them seriously ill (see `appetite-loss`).
