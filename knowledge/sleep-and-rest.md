# Sleep and rest

Pets rest far more than people. Adult dogs often spend 12 to 14 hours a day sleeping or resting, and cats 12 to 16 hours. Puppies, kittens and older pets rest even more.

Short periods of more rest after a busy day are normal.

**Signs that rest may mean a problem:**

- a lasting rise in lying time together with less walking
- restlessness, pacing or getting up and down at night
- difficulty getting comfortable, which can be a sign of pain
- fast breathing while asleep (see `breathing-difficulty`)

Collar data shows trends over days better than single readings.
