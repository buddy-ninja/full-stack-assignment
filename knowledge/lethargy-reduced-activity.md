# Lethargy and reduced activity

A pet that is quieter than usual for a day, after a busy day or in hot weather, is usually fine. A drop that lasts several days, or comes with other signs, needs attention.

**Collar data can help.** A steady fall in walking or standing minutes over three or more days, with more time lying down, is worth noting even if the pet still eats. Compare against the pet's own normal, not against other pets.

**Book a vet visit within a few days if** reduced activity lasts more than two to three days with no clear reason.

**Contact a vet the same day if** reduced activity comes with:

- not eating or drinking
- vomiting, diarrhoea or weight loss
- limping, stiffness or reluctance to jump
- hiding (in cats) or signs of pain

Cats hide illness well, so a quiet cat deserves a closer look.
