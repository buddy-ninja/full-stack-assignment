# Drinking and urinating more

Drinking more on a hot day or after exercise is normal. A lasting increase in thirst, often with more urination, can be an early sign of conditions such as kidney disease, diabetes or hormone problems.

**See a vet within a few days if:**

- your pet drinks noticeably more than usual for several days
- you are filling the water bowl much more often
- a house-trained pet starts having accidents

If you can, measure how much water the pet drinks in 24 hours and bring that number to the vet.

A male cat straining to urinate is a different problem and an emergency (see `urinary-blockage-cats`).
