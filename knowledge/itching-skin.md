# Itching and skin problems

Common causes of itching are fleas, allergies (to food or the environment) and skin infections.

**Start by checking for fleas,** even in indoor pets. Look for small dark specks in the coat that turn red on damp paper.

**See a vet if:**

- itching keeps the pet awake or causes sores
- there is hair loss, redness, scabs or a bad smell
- the pet keeps licking or chewing one spot

Keep up regular parasite prevention (see `parasite-prevention`). Do not use dog flea products on cats: some contain permethrin, which is toxic to cats.
