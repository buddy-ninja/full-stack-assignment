# Bloat (GDV) in dogs

Gastric dilatation-volvulus (GDV), or bloat, happens when a dog's stomach fills with gas and can twist. **It is an emergency. Without fast surgery, it is usually fatal.**

**Contact a vet now** if a dog:

- retches or tries to vomit but brings nothing up
- has a swollen, tight belly
- is restless, pacing or cannot get comfortable
- drools heavily or breathes fast

Large, deep-chested breeds are at highest risk, but any dog can be affected. Eating one large meal a day, eating fast, and exercise straight after a meal increase the risk.

Do not wait to see if it settles.
