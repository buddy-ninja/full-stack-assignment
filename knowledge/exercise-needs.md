# Exercise and activity needs

**Dogs** need daily exercise suited to their breed, age and health. Most adult dogs do well with 30 to 90 minutes of walking or play spread across the day. Working and hunting breeds such as beagles often need more.

**Cats** are active in short bursts. Indoor cats benefit from two or three short play sessions a day with wand toys, plus places to climb.

Changes matter more than absolute numbers. A pet that is suddenly much less active than its usual pattern may be unwell or in pain (see `lethargy-reduced-activity`).

Build up exercise gradually after illness, in hot weather, and for overweight or older pets.
