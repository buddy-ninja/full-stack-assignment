# Vaccination basics

Vaccines protect pets against serious infectious diseases.

- **Puppies and kittens** need a first course, usually starting at 6 to 8 weeks with boosters a few weeks apart.
- **Adults** need boosters. How often depends on the vaccine and local disease risk; some are yearly and some every three years.
- **Indoor cats** still benefit from core vaccines.

Mild tiredness or a small lump at the injection site for a day or two is common.

**Contact a vet now** if, after a vaccine, a pet has facial swelling, hives, vomiting, collapse or trouble breathing.

Your vet will recommend a schedule based on where you live and your pet's lifestyle.
