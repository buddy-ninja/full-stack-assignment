# Diarrhoea in dogs and cats

Mild, short-lived diarrhoea is often caused by a food change, scavenging or stress. Many cases settle within 24 to 48 hours.

**Home care:** keep water available, feed small bland meals, and avoid treats and new foods until stools are normal again.

**Contact a vet the same day if:**

- diarrhoea lasts more than 48 hours
- there is a lot of blood, or stools are black and tarry
- the pet is also vomiting, not eating, or seems weak
- the pet is a puppy, a kitten, or elderly, because they dehydrate faster

Note how often it happens and what the stools look like. A photo can help the vet.
