# Ear problems

Signs of ear trouble include head shaking, scratching at the ear, a bad smell, discharge, or redness inside the ear flap.

Dogs with floppy ears and dogs that swim often are more prone to ear infections. In cats, ear mites are a common cause, especially in young cats.

**See a vet** if signs last more than a day or two. Do not put cotton buds deep into the ear, and do not use drops without a vet's advice: if the eardrum is damaged, some drops can cause harm.

**Contact a vet the same day** if the pet tilts its head, loses balance or walks in circles.
