# Limping and joint pain

Limping can come from a sore paw pad, a nail injury, a sprain, or longer-term joint problems such as arthritis.

**Home care for a mild limp:** rest, short lead walks only, and check the paw for cuts, thorns or broken nails.

**See a vet if:**

- the limp lasts more than 2 days, or keeps coming back
- the pet will not put any weight on the leg
- a joint is swollen or hot
- the pet cries out when the leg is touched

**Do not give human pain relief.** Ibuprofen, paracetamol and aspirin can be poisonous to pets (see `toxin-ingestion`). Ask a vet about safe pain relief.

Older pets with arthritis often show it as stiffness after rest, reluctance to jump, or less walking time rather than an obvious limp.
