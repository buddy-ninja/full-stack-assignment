# Loss of appetite

Skipping one meal is not unusual, especially in hot weather or after a change in routine.

**Cats should not go without food for long.** A cat that has eaten nothing for more than 24 hours should see a vet. Fasting can trigger a serious liver condition, particularly in overweight cats.

**Dogs:** contact a vet if a dog refuses food for more than 24 to 48 hours, or sooner if it is also vomiting, lethargic or in pain.

Check the mouth for broken teeth or sore gums, since dental pain is a common reason for eating less (see `dental-care`).
