# Seizures

During a seizure a pet may fall over, paddle its legs, drool, lose bladder control or be unaware of its surroundings.

**During the seizure:**

- keep your hands away from the mouth; pets do not swallow their tongue
- move furniture away and keep the room dim and quiet
- time it

**Contact a vet now if:**

- this is the first seizure
- a seizure lasts more than 5 minutes
- there is more than one seizure in 24 hours
- the pet does not return to normal within an hour
- the pet may have eaten a toxin (see `toxin-ingestion`)

After a seizure, pets are often confused, wobbly or temporarily blind for a while. Keep them somewhere safe and quiet.
