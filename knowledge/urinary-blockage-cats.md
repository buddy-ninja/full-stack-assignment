# Urinary blockage in male cats

Male cats can develop a blockage that stops them passing urine. **This is an emergency and can be fatal within a day or two.**

**Contact a vet now** if a cat:

- strains in the litter box and produces little or no urine
- goes back to the litter box again and again
- cries while trying to urinate
- licks its genital area a lot
- vomits or becomes very quiet while showing any of the signs above

Cats who have had urinary problems before, are overweight, live indoors or eat only dry food are at higher risk. Stress can also trigger it.

Female cats with urinary signs should also see a vet soon, though a full blockage is much rarer.
