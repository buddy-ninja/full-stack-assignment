# Vomiting in dogs and cats

A single episode of vomiting in an otherwise bright pet is common. It often follows eating too fast, eating grass or a sudden change of food. Cats may also bring up hairballs.

**Home care for a single episode:** withhold food for a few hours, keep fresh water available, then offer a small bland meal. Watch for 24 hours.

**Contact a vet the same day if:**

- vomiting happens more than twice in a day, or keeps coming back over several days
- the pet is lethargic, hiding or refusing water
- there is blood, or material that looks like coffee grounds
- the pet is very young, old, or has a known medical condition

**Contact a vet now** if a dog is retching without bringing anything up and the belly looks swollen (see `bloat-gdv`), or if you suspect the pet swallowed a toxin or a foreign object (see `toxin-ingestion`).
