# Dental care

Most adult dogs and cats have some dental disease. It is painful but often hidden, because pets keep eating.

**Signs:** bad breath, red or bleeding gums, brown tartar, dropping food, chewing on one side, or pawing at the mouth.

**Prevention:**

- daily tooth brushing with pet toothpaste (never human toothpaste, which can contain xylitol)
- dental chews or diets designed for teeth
- a dental check at each yearly vet visit

A broken tooth or a swelling on the face should be seen by a vet within a few days.
